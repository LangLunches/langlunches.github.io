When & Where

  • Time: Tuesdays 12:00 – 13:00 (Biweekly)
  • Location: Building C7 2, Room -1.05
  • Format: ~20 min talk, followed by Q&A and Bring your own Lunch :)

How to present

The talks are informal and flexible. You can present work-in-progress, recent preprints, or already-published papers. We're here to promote cross-group collaboration and share ideas, so come present your research or simply listen in!

To present, just drop your name and topic into any open slot on the shared Excel sheet. We will update this website's schedule to match!
